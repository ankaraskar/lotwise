ABOUT LOTWISE

Official sources. Clear limitations.

What it does

Queries official GIS address points, presents available zoning standards and indexes official research passages.

What it cannot certify

Legal lot limits, site-specific amendments, appeals, permitted uses or definitive OBC compliance. Unsupported findings remain marked for review.

Privacy

Client project details remain in your browser. Addresses and coordinates are sent to the corresponding government's GIS service.

Open source

Lotwise is a free research prototype hosted on GitHub Pages. Its official documentation index updates through GitHub Actions.
