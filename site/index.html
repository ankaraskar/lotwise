LIMITATIONS & REVIEW

Trace the source.
Review the judgment.

Designed to help an architectural project team prepare for an informed professional review.

What works

Official address-point GIS lookup, curated Toronto CR SS2 references and Brantford MCC use-specific standards, indicative Brantford non-residential parking rates, Ontario Building Code scope screening, and scheduled government document indexing.

What remains unverified

Legal parcel lines, property-specific exceptions and appeals, proposed uses, complete parking totals, building-code applicability and design compliance. Unknown items are not replaced with guesses.

Privacy and cost

Static GitHub Pages website: no account or paid API. Address or coordinates go to the official municipal GIS service; client briefs are not uploaded to our servers. Avoid placing confidential briefs in public GitHub issues.

Dates and amendments

Government law consolidations can lag amendments and decisions. Always open the provided current official record before determining legal applicability.
