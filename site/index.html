Question the conclusion.

Lotwise is a research aid, not a replacement for municipal review or licensed professionals.

What the site does now

Queries specified official GIS services in the browser, requires confirmation of address candidates, provides a bounded reference to Brantford MCC standards, estimates base parking for supported uses, and searches a GitHub Actions–generated official-document index.

What it cannot establish yet

It cannot verify legal parcel limits, all amendments and appeals, permission for a proposed use, required parking after exceptions, or full Building Code compliance. Other zones are explicitly marked as not yet structured.

Privacy & open source

Your client brief stays in your browser while you use this page. We do not have a user account system, analytics, or a paid AI API. Official GIS requests necessarily transmit the searched address or coordinates to the municipality's published GIS service.

Why not ask AI to guess?

Official-document extracts are shown with the original URL, page and last successful index date. The system never silently substitutes a general rule when the applicable special provisions or circumstances are unknown.
